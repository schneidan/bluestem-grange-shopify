About

Bluestem Grange is a small soap shop in Franktown, Colorado, named for the tallgrass that still holds this prairie and the old grange halls that gathered it.

We have been making bar soap here since 2020 — cold-process, poured in small batches, cut by hand. No pumps, no plastic bottles. Just a bar that earns its keep at the sink.
