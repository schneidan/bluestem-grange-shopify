Ingredients

Every bar starts the same way: plant oils, lye, water, and time. We use a cold-process method, so the soap cures for several weeks before it leaves Franktown.

The usual base is olive oil, coconut oil, and shea butter. Some bars add colloidal oatmeal or honey. Scent comes from essential oils — cedar, sage, lavender, rose geranium, mint, pine — never a wall of fragrance oil.

Each product page lists that bar’s extras. If you have a sensitivity, write us before you order.
